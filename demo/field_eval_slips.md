# MedPrep Field Evaluation — Participant Instructions & Blind Mistake Slips

## Protocol (for the Proctor)
1. **Freeze config first:** Do not change `live` thresholds after the first field run starts.
2. **Do not coach:** Hand the participant the printed `demo/medprep_card.md` and one folded slip below.
3. **Blind slip:** Ask the participant to pick or write **one intended mistake** (or `NONE (perfect run)`), fold the slip, and keep it hidden until after the run.
4. **Run live:** Click **Load MedPrep Demo** → **Start Live Monitor** in `/static/live.html`. When they finish, hold the open organizer under the camera and click **Finish & Verify Organizer**.
5. **Label & unseal:** Open `/static/review.html`, inspect the captured 6-frame tick strips and alert timestamps, label each step `ok` / `error` (category), then open the folded slip. Add any missed intended mistakes and keep any unintended mistakes. Click **Save Field Label & Rescore** (writes `data/field_gold.jsonl`).
